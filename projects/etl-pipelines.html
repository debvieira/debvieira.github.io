Overview

Reliable pipelines adapted to distinct sources and business rules.

I develop and maintain ETL/ELT pipelines for multiple clients, supporting the complete journey from collection and transformation to validation and loading. Each workflow is adapted to its source systems, expected formats and business requirements.

Approach

Quality built into the flow.

The pipelines include checks for missing information, invalid values and inconsistencies before data reaches downstream reports and business processes. When issues are identified, they are documented and communicated so corrections can happen at the right point in the workflow.

Outcome

A maintainable delivery process designed to prevent unreliable data from moving downstream.
