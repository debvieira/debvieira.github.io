Tableau to QuickSight
Cloud Migration

Role
Data Analyst / Analytics Engineer
Scope
30+ dashboards
Toolkit
AWS Glue, PySpark, Athena, S3, SQL
Collaboration
International stakeholders

Challenge

Modernize analytics without losing trust in the numbers.

The initiative involved migrating more than 30 dashboards from Tableau to Amazon QuickSight while mapping and centralizing multiple data sources in AWS. The work extended beyond visualization: analytical datasets, transformations and business rules also had to be reconstructed and validated.

Approach

Rebuild the data foundation alongside the dashboards.

I developed data pipelines and analytical tables with AWS Glue and PySpark, queried data through Athena and organized source discovery with Glue Crawlers. I also rebuilt SQL logic, investigated discrepancies and worked with international stakeholders to align metric definitions.

Outcome

A more reliable, scalable and maintainable cloud analytics environment supporting the migrated reporting experience.
