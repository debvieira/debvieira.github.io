Context

Turn live application activity into a clear view of product performance.

Product and business teams needed a reliable way to follow application usage, understand feature adoption and identify meaningful changes without depending on manual reporting.

Approach

Bring operational indicators together in one monitoring experience.

I integrated application data, structured the metrics and developed a real-time dashboard with filters and trend views. The result gave stakeholders a consistent way to monitor activity and investigate changes in user behavior.

Outcome

Faster visibility into application usage and stronger data support for product and operational decisions.
